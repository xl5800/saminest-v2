Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Chinese Tattoo Generator ("the app") is made by XUELONG WANG ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. In short: your photos never leave your phone, we show no ads, and we do not sell or share your data.

What we do not collect

  • Photos and camera images. The try-on camera and any photo you pick are processed only on your device. They are never uploaded. An image is saved to your Photos only when you tap Save.
  • Payment details. Apple handles all payments. We never see your card number or billing address.
  • Location, contacts, or advertising IDs.
  • Analytics or ads. The app has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

How data is stored and who processes it

Your account data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. Purchases are processed by Apple and verified by RevenueCat, which receives your random account ID and your purchase history so it can tell the app whether Pro is active. Both process data on our behalf. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple handles that sign-in under Apple's privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal information or share it with anyone for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Your style settings (Simplified or Traditional, line weight) and a copy of your favorites are also stored on your device.

How long we keep it

  • Account data (favorites, history): until you delete your account.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed to provide Pro and to meet tax and accounting rules. Apple keeps its own record of your purchases.
  • Support messages: as long as needed to handle your request; when you delete your account, they are kept without your account ID.
  • What you typed on the Create screen: kept with your account until you delete it; after that, kept without any account ID for up to 12 months to improve the phrase library.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Me → account icon → Delete account (guests see Delete my data). This permanently deletes your account, favorites and history. Deleting the app alone does not delete a guest account's data on our servers. If you already deleted the app, reinstall it and send us a message from Me → Contact support saying roughly when you used it, and we will delete the old guest account's data for you.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by sending a message from the app (Me → Contact support). We will respond within 30 days. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by California law.

Children

Chinese Tattoo Generator is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
