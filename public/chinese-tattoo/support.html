Frequently asked questions

Is the Chinese written by AI?

No. Every phrase comes from our reviewed library. Software only helps match your words to phrases that already exist; it never writes new Chinese.

Should I still check with my tattoo artist?

Yes. Bring the image you save from the app, and ask your artist to copy it exactly. Small changes to a stroke can change a character. Sizes in the app are approximate.

Simplified or Traditional?

Traditional is used in Taiwan, Hong Kong and many older overseas communities. Simplified is used in mainland China and has fewer strokes, so it stays clearer at small sizes. You can switch at any time.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The camera and your photos are used only on your phone. A picture is saved to your Photos only when you tap Save.

What does Pro include?

HD downloads to show your tattoo artist, saving and sharing try-on photos, and unlimited favorites. Searching, meanings, pinyin and the live try-on preview are free.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Chinese Tattoo Generator and tap Cancel. You can also go to Me → Pro is active in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I paid but Pro isn't unlocked, or I got a new phone.

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with. If that doesn't help, send us a message from the app.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I got a new phone. How do I keep my favorites?

Go to Me → account icon and tap Sign in with Apple on your old phone first, then sign in with the same Apple ID on the new one. Guest accounts cannot be moved to another phone.

How do I delete my account and data?

Me → account icon → Delete account (guests see Delete my data). This removes your favorites and history for good. See our Privacy Policy.

I didn't find a phrase for my story.

Try describing it another way, in English. If nothing fits, send us a message; we read every one and use them to add new phrases.

I think a meaning is wrong.

Please tell us which phrase and why. We review every report.
